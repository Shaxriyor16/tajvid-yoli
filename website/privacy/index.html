1. Umumiy

Bu siyosat «Tajvid yo'li» mobil ilovasi (keyingi o'rinlarda — «Ilova») qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi, ulardan nima uchun foydalanishi va sizda qanday tanlovlar borligini tushuntiradi. Ilovadan foydalanib, siz shu siyosat bilan tanishganingizni tasdiqlaysiz. Savollar uchun: support@tajvidyoli.uz.

Qisqacha: hisob ma'lumotlari Google Firebase'da saqlanadi; o'quv natijalari qurilmangizda qoladi; ovoz faqat siz AI mashqini boshlaganingizda tahlil uchun yuboriladi; hisobni ilovaning o'zidan o'chirishingiz mumkin; hozircha reklama yo'q.

3. Ovoz yozuvi va sun'iy intellekt

AI mashqida yoki «sura aniqlash»da mikrofon faqat siz tugmani bosganingizdan keyin ishlaydi. Yozuv qisqa bo'ladi va Firebase AI Logic orqali Google Gemini xizmatiga tahlil qilish uchun yuboriladi. Natija (eshitilgan matn, taxminiy baho, tavsiya) ekranda ko'rsatiladi.

  • Vaqtinchalik audio fayl tahlil tugagach qurilmangizdan o'chiriladi. Biz ovoz yozuvlarini o'z serverlarimizda saqlamaymiz.
  • Google xizmatni taqdim etish uchun yozuvni o'z shartlariga muvofiq qayta ishlaydi. Bepul tarifda yuborilgan ma'lumotlar Google mahsulotlarini yaxshilash uchun ishlatilishi mumkinligi ilovada ham eslatib o'tilgan.
  • AI bahosi taxminiy. U eshitilgan so'zlarni solishtiradi, maxraj va tajvidni ovozdan to'liq baholamaydi va ustoz bahosining o'rnini bosmaydi.
  • Mikrofon ruxsatini istalgan vaqtda qurilma sozlamalaridan o'chirib qo'yishingiz mumkin; bunda AI mashqlari ishlamaydi, qolgan qismlar ishlayveradi.

4. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

  • Google Firebase — hisob, ma'lumotlar bazasi, fayl saqlash, tahlil, nosozlik hisobotlari, App Check va AI Logic. Google Maxfiylik siyosati.
  • Google bilan kirish va Apple bilan kirish — tanlasangiz, ular sizning ismingiz va emailingizni bizga uzatadi. Apple maxfiylik siyosati.
  • AlQuran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) — Qur'on matni, tarjima va lotincha o'qilishi; tilovat audiosi cdn.islamic.network orqali yuklanadi. Bu so'rovlarda qurilmangizning IP manzili ko'rinishi mumkin; shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz yuborilmaydi.

5. Reklama

Ilovaning hozirgi versiyasida reklama ko'rsatilmaydi va reklama maqsadida ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi. Kelajakda reklama qo'shilsa, avval ushbu siyosat yangilanadi.

6. Saqlash muddati va o'chirish

  • Hisob ma'lumotlari hisobingiz mavjud bo'lguncha saqlanadi.
  • Hisobni o'chirish: ilovada Sozlamalar › Hisob › Hisobni o'chirish. Bunda hisobingiz, profil ma'lumotlaringiz va profil rasmingiz serverlardan o'chiriladi, qurilmadagi o'quv natijalari tozalanadi. Batafsil: Hisobni o'chirish.
  • Ilovani o'chirsangiz, qurilmadagi o'quv ma'lumotlari ham o'chadi.
  • Google Analytics/Crashlytics'ning umumlashtirilgan yoki anonim ma'lumotlari Google belgilagan muddat davomida saqlanishi mumkin.

7. Huquqlaringiz

Siz o'zingiz haqingizdagi ma'lumotlarni ko'rish, tuzatish yoki o'chirishni so'rash huquqiga egasiz. Hisobni ilova ichida o'chirishingiz mumkin. Boshqa so'rovlar uchun support@tajvidyoli.uz manziliga ro'yxatdan o'tgan emailingizdan yozing. Mikrofon va bildirishnoma ruxsatlarini qurilma sozlamalaridan qaytarib olishingiz mumkin.

8. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalardan ataylab shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'maydi. Agar bolangiz ilovada ma'lumot kiritganini bilsangiz, bizga yozing — tegishli ma'lumotlarni o'chiramiz.

9. Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar shifrlangan (HTTPS) ulanish orqali uzatiladi. Ma'lumotlar bazasi qoidalari har bir foydalanuvchiga faqat o'z hujjatiga kirishga ruxsat beradi, so'rovlar App Check bilan himoyalangan. Hech bir tizim mutlaq xavfsiz emas, ammo biz ma'lumotlaringizni himoyalash uchun oqilona choralarni ko'ramiz. Google serverlari turli mamlakatlarda joylashgan bo'lishi mumkin.

10. Saytdagi «Chiqqanda xabar bering» formasi

Saytimizda ixtiyoriy forma bor: unga emailingizni kiritib, ilova chiqqanda xabar olishni so'rashingiz mumkin. Email brauzeringizdan FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) xizmati orqali bizning pochta qutimizga yuboriladi va faqat ilova chiqishi haqida xabar berish uchun ishlatiladi; uni reklama uchun uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz. Xabarnomadan voz kechish yoki emailingizni o'chirishni support@tajvidyoli.uz ga yozib so'rashingiz mumkin. Forma bilan birga yuborilgan til va sahifa manzili ham bizga yetib keladi.

11. O'zgarishlar va aloqa

Siyosatni yangilab turishimiz mumkin; muhim o'zgarishlarda sahifadagi sanani yangilaymiz. Savol va so'rovlar: support@tajvidyoli.uz yoki Telegram: @m24_shaxa.

1. Overview

This policy explains what data the “Tajvid Yo'li” mobile app (the “App”) collects, why it uses it, and the choices you have. By using the App you confirm that you have read this policy. Questions: support@tajvidyoli.uz.

In short: account data is stored in Google Firebase; learning progress stays on your device; your voice is sent only when you start an AI exercise; you can delete your account from inside the app; there are currently no ads.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photo library or financial information. If you choose a profile photo, only the file you select is uploaded.

3. Voice recording and artificial intelligence

In an AI exercise or “find a surah”, the microphone is used only after you tap the button. The short recording is sent to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic for analysis. The result (recognised text, approximate score, feedback) is shown on screen.

  • The temporary audio file is deleted from your device when analysis finishes. We do not store voice recordings on our own servers.
  • Google processes the recording to provide the service under its own terms. On the free tier, submitted data may be used to improve Google products, as the App also notes.
  • The AI score is approximate. It compares recognised words, does not fully judge makhraj or tajweed from audio, and does not replace a teacher.
  • You can turn off microphone permission at any time in device settings; AI exercises then stop working while the rest of the App continues to work.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — accounts, database, file storage, analytics, crash reports, App Check and AI Logic. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple — if you choose them, they pass your name and email to us. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • AlQuran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) — Qur'an text, translation and transliteration; recitation audio is loaded from cdn.islamic.network. Your device's IP address may be visible in these requests; no personal data is sent.

5. Advertising

The current version of the App shows no ads and collects no data for advertising. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Deleting your account: in the App, Settings › Account › Delete account. This removes your account, profile data and profile photo from our servers and clears learning data on the device. Details: Delete account.
  • If you uninstall the App, the learning data on your device is removed too.
  • Aggregated or anonymous Google Analytics/Crashlytics data may be retained for the period set by Google.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. You can delete your account inside the App. For other requests, write to support@tajvidyoli.uz from your registered email. You can withdraw microphone and notification permissions in device settings.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Database rules let each user access only their own record, and requests are protected by App Check. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data. Google's servers may be located in different countries.

10. “Notify me at launch” form on the website

Our website has an optional form where you can leave your email to be told when the app is released. The email is sent from your browser to our mailbox through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co) and is used only to tell you about the release; we do not give it to third parties for advertising. To unsubscribe or have your email deleted, write to support@tajvidyoli.uz. The page language and page address are sent along with the form.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; for material changes we will update the date on this page. Questions and requests: support@tajvidyoli.uz or Telegram @m24_shaxa.
