Sun'iy intellekt haqida ochiq gapiramizHonest about AI

AI — yordamchi, ustoz emasAI is a helper, not a teacher

Talaffuz bahosi AI eshitgan so'zlarni solishtirishga asoslangan. Maxraj va tajvidni ovozdan to'liq baholay olmaydi.The pronunciation score compares the words the AI recognised. It cannot fully judge articulation points (makhraj) or tajweed from audio.

  • Baho taxminiy — chuqur tekshiruv uchun malakali ustozga murojaat qiling.The score is approximate — see a qualified teacher for in-depth checking.
  • Ovoz faqat siz mashqni boshlaganingizda tahlil uchun Google Gemini xizmatiga yuboriladi.Audio is sent to Google Gemini for analysis only when you start an exercise.
  • Vaqtinchalik audio fayl tahlildan so'ng qurilmangizdan o'chiriladi.The temporary audio file is deleted from your device after analysis.
  • Yutuq sertifikati faqat ilova ichidagi rag'bat — rasmiy ijozat emas.The achievement certificate is in-app motivation only — not an official ijazah.

Savol-javobFAQ

Ko'p so'raladigan savollarFrequently asked questions

Tajvid yo'li nima?What is Tajvid Yo'li?

Qur'onni to'g'ri va tajvid qoidalari bilan o'qishni o'rganish uchun mo'ljallangan ta'lim ilovasi: alifbo, harakatlar, tajvid kursi, suralar va AI mashqlari.A learning app for reading the Qur'an correctly and with tajweed: alphabet, vowel marks, a tajweed course, surahs and AI practice.

AI orqali tekshirish qanday ishlaydi?How does AI checking work?

Iborani o'qiysiz, ilova ovozni tahlil uchun Google Gemini xizmatiga yuboradi va eshitilgan so'zlarni solishtirib, taxminiy baho va tavsiya ko'rsatadi. Bu ustoz tekshiruvining o'rnini bosmaydi.You recite a phrase, the app sends the audio to Google Gemini for analysis and shows an approximate score and feedback based on the recognised words. It does not replace a teacher.

Internet bo'lmasa ham ishlaydimi?Does it work offline?

Ha, darslar va mashqlarning ko'pi offline ishlaydi. Ba'zi materiallarni birinchi marta yuklash uchun, shuningdek AI va tilovat audiosi uchun internet kerak.Yes, most lessons and exercises work offline. Internet is needed to download some materials the first time, and for AI features and recitation audio.

Sertifikat qanday olinadi?How do I get a certificate?

Kamida bitta darsni muvaffaqiyatli yakunlagach, ilova ichida yutuq sertifikati paydo bo'ladi. U motivatsion xarakterda va rasmiy ijozat yoki diniy sertifikat emas.After successfully finishing at least one lesson, an achievement certificate appears in the app. It is for motivation only and is not an official ijazah or religious certificate.

Ma'lumotlarim xavfsizmi? Hisobni o'chirsam nima bo'ladi?Is my data safe? What happens if I delete my account?

Hisob ma'lumotlari Google Firebase'da saqlanadi va faqat o'zingiz o'qiy olasiz. Hisobni ilovaning o'zidan o'chirsangiz, hisob, profil va profil rasmi serverlardan o'chiriladi. Maxfiylik siyosatini o'qing.Account data is stored in Google Firebase and only you can read it. If you delete your account in the app, your account, profile and profile photo are removed from our servers. Read the Privacy Policy.

Qaysi qurilmalarda ishlaydi?Which devices are supported?

Hozir Android uchun tayyorlangan. Boshqa platformalar haqida yangiliklarni shu sahifada e'lon qilamiz.It is currently built for Android. We will announce other platforms on this page.

Savolingiz yoki taklifingiz bormi?Questions or suggestions?

Ilova chiqishi, xatolar haqida xabar berish yoki fikr bildirish uchun bizga yozing — har bir xatni o'qiymiz.Write to us about the launch, to report a problem or to share feedback — we read every message.
