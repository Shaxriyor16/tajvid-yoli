Hisobni o'chirishDelete your account

Tajvid yo'li hisobingizni va unga bog'liq ma'lumotlarni o'zingiz o'chirishingiz mumkin.You can delete your Tajvid Yo'li account and the data linked to it yourself.

Ilova ichida o'chirish (eng tez yo'l)

  1. Ilovani oching va Profil bo'limiga o'ting.
  2. Yuqoridagi sozlamalar (shesternya) belgisini bosing.
  3. Hisob guruhida «Hisobni o'chirish»ni tanlang.
  4. Ogohlantirishni o'qib, «Hisobni o'chirish»ni tasdiqlang.
Xavfsizlik uchun o'chirish oldidan yaqinda (taxminan 5 daqiqa ichida) tizimga kirgan bo'lishingiz kerak. Ilova «qayta kiring» desa — chiqib, qayta kiring va yana urinib ko'ring.

Nima o'chiriladi

  • Kirish hisobingiz (Firebase Authentication);
  • profil ma'lumotlaringiz: email, ism, familiya, yosh, kirish usuli (Cloud Firestore);
  • profil rasmingiz (Cloud Storage);
  • hisobingizga bog'langan xizmat hisoblagichlari (mavjud bo'lsa);
  • qurilmangizdagi o'quv natijalari (ilova tomonidan tozalanadi).

Nima qolishi mumkin

  • Google Analytics/Crashlytics'ning umumlashtirilgan yoki anonim ma'lumotlari Google belgilagan muddat davomida saqlanishi mumkin.
  • Qonun talab qilgan hollarda saqlanishi shart bo'lgan yozuvlar (bo'lsa).

O'chirish qaytarib bo'lmaydi: hisob va ma'lumotlarni tiklab bo'lmaydi.

Ilovani o'rnatib bo'lmasa yoki kira olmasangiz

Bizga support@tajvidyoli.uz manziliga, hisobingizga ro'yxatdan o'tgan emaildan, mavzusi «Hisobni o'chirish» bo'lgan xat yozing. Shaxsingizni tasdiqlagach, hisob va ma'lumotlarni o'chiramiz hamda sizga xabar beramiz.

Ma'lumotlarni qayta ishlash haqida batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati.

Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the settings (gear) icon at the top.
  3. In the Account group choose “Delete account”.
  4. Read the warning and confirm “Delete account”.
For security you must have signed in recently (within about 5 minutes) before deleting. If the app asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in and retry.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication);
  • your profile data: email, first and last name, age, sign-in method (Cloud Firestore);
  • your profile photo (Cloud Storage);
  • service counters linked to your account (if any);
  • learning data on your device (cleared by the app).

What may remain

  • Aggregated or anonymous Google Analytics/Crashlytics data may be retained for the period set by Google.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, if any.

Deletion is permanent: the account and data cannot be restored.

If you cannot install the app or sign in

Email support@tajvidyoli.uz from the email registered to your account with the subject “Delete my account”. After verifying your identity we will delete the account and data and let you know.
